# Dependencies

This release depends on Lean 4 and the pinned mathlib repository. Their
source and caches are fetched during the build and remain separate from
this archive. Both projects distribute their source under Apache-2.0;
consult the exact dependency revisions for their complete license notices.

* Lean: https://github.com/leanprover/lean4/tree/v4.35.0
* mathlib: https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/tree/56a005855ea17e0b0958f73ec801ae140b0a1679
